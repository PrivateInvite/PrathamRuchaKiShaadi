Prathamesh & Rucha — hosted invitation
======================================

DO NOT UPLOAD THIS FILE to the repository. It is notes for you.
Upload only:  index.html, robots.txt, and the folder a/

The passphrase is the one you already have. It is deliberately not
written down in this folder, because the repository is public.


What is in the folder
---------------------
index.html   the gate and the whole invitation, encrypted   38 KB
a/*.bin      one encrypted picture each                    975 KB
robots.txt   keeps search engines out

A guest downloads 38 KB and sees the passphrase box almost at once.
Each picture is fetched and decrypted only as they scroll to it.
Nothing here is readable without the passphrase — not the names, not
the venue, not the artwork. The repository is safe to be public.


Uploading to github.com/shwetalibckup-site/wedding
--------------------------------------------------
Pages is already switched on for this repo, so there is nothing to
configure. Upload index.html and the a/ folder TOGETHER, in one
commit — if the new index.html lands without a/, the site will show
the gate and then a page with no pictures until a/ arrives.

  Add file -> Upload files -> drag in index.html, robots.txt and a/
  -> Commit.

Give it a minute, then open

  https://shwetalibckup-site.github.io/wedding/

on your phone, with the passphrase, before sending it to anyone.


Changing something later
------------------------
Every change — a time, a name, a new artwork — is made in the source
and the folder is rebuilt; the published files cannot be edited
because they are encrypted. Send the change and you get a fresh
folder to upload over the old one. The URL stays the same, so anyone
who already has the link gets the update.


If the pictures do not appear
-----------------------------
This folder only works from a web address. Double-clicking
index.html shows the gate and then a page with no artwork — that is
expected. Keep the old single-file invite as your offline copy.


Two things that are not the website's job
-----------------------------------------
The link will be forwarded; that is what it is for, and the
passphrase travels with it. It stops a stranger finding the invite by
accident. It does not stop anyone who wants to be at the venue. A
guest list at the entrance, someone watching the gift table all
evening, and jewellery in a locked room are what actually matter —
and the reception is the event to put them at.
